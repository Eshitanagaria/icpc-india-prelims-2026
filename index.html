Unofficial dashboard built from the tentative ranklist released by ICPC India. Submission links go to CodeChef's status page for the ICPC India 2026 contest, filtered to that team and problem. Tied teams share a rank. The institute topper tag marks the best-ranked team from each college. College names that differed only in spelling or capitalisation were merged. Institute type is read from the college name: IIT, NIT or IIIT, everything else is Other. Regional tags match on team name and college, so a college spelled differently in the two lists can be missed, and two teams with the same name at one college are both tagged.

Every team registered for this regional, from its registration list. A team is shown with its prelims rank when its name and college match a team on the ranklist. Teams without a match are not on the ranklist, which only lists teams that solved at least one problem. The institute topper tag and the college rank come from that ranklist: the topper is the best-ranked team from its college, and the college rank counts every ranklist team from the same college, not only the ones registered here. Teams not on the ranklist have neither. A college spelled differently in the two lists can be missed.
